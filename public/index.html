    How to play

    • On your turn pick one of your two cards, then move any of your pieces to a square the card shows (relative to the light centre square).
    • Landing on an enemy piece captures it. You can't land on your own pieces.
    • The card you used becomes the spare; you take the previous spare, and your opponent gets yours after their move.
    • Win by capturing the enemy master (crown), or by moving your master onto the enemy temple — the ringed square in the middle of their home row.
